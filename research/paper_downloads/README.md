# Papers on the student moving away from the teacher in RL

The PDFs are grouped by their role in the project. Titles and links are in `sources.tsv`; `download_status.tsv` records the outcome of the automatic check (a file is included only if it is identified as a PDF).

## Folders

- `01_imitation_gap`: learning with observations or students less informed than the teacher.
- `02_suboptimal_demonstrations`: methods that go beyond suboptimal demonstrations or experts.
- `03_teacher_student`: works already cited on the teacher → student transfer, and the unbiased asymmetric critic.
- `04_racing`: racing applications and references.
- `05_coach_tools`: tools for the coach phase and for offline/online RL.
- `06_corrections_and_notes`: notes on the bibliographic corrections.
- `07_evaluation_methodology`: statistical methodology of RL experiments (seeds, confidence intervals, reporting).

## Access note

Nature papers are included only when the PDF is served freely by the publisher or the authors. For GT Sophy the PDF hosted by an author is included; the correct DOI is `10.1038/s41586-021-04357-7`.

Of 29 papers identified, 28 PDFs are included (the 25th, Baisero & Amato, was added after reading Scaffolder, which cites it for the asymmetric critic; the 26th-29th, on evaluation methodology, when the criteria of the sensor plan student were revised). The only PDF not included is *Impossibly Good Experts and How to Follow Them*: OpenReview rejected the automated download with an anti-bot check; its note keeps the official link.
